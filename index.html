In this section, you will act as the primary decision-maker choosing an outside English program for your child. You will be presented with 6 hypothetical choice scenarios. Conditions held constant across all program options:

To ensure a fair comparison, all educational options in this survey strictly adhere to the following 5 fixed standards:

  • 1. Session schedule: Eight 90-minute sessions per period, typically 2 sessions per week.
  • 2. Class size: Maximum of 20 children per class.
  • 3. Teacher standards: The same stated teacher qualification and experience requirements across all options.
  • 4. Facilities & safety: The same stated basic classroom equipment, learning materials, and safety conditions.
  • 5. Pricing transparency: No additional compulsory fees beyond those shown (no hidden registration, textbook, or uniform fees).
